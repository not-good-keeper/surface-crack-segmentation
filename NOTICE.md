## Why non-commercial

The licence is not a preference. The corpus mixes CC0, CC-BY, CC-BY-NC and research-only
sources, and the most restrictive terms present are non-commercial — MVTec AD,
KolektorSDD2 and the casting set are all CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0. A model trained on
non-commercial data is not automatically free of the non-commercial condition, so the
restriction propagates to `data/export/model.onnx` and to anything derived from it.

Applying the same terms repository-wide keeps the licence honest rather than leaving the
model under one set of terms and the code under another.

## Per-source attribution

`docs/ATTRIBUTION.md` lists every source in the corpus, its licence, its credit line and
exactly what was taken from it. Anyone redistributing this work, or the model, must
carry those credits forward and re-check the per-source terms — several sources are
research-only and some were deliberately excluded from redistribution.
